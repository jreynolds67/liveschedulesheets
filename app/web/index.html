Sheet preview & row mapping

Rows are detected automatically for each tab. Check the highlighted rows and the Parses as line. If a row is wrong, choose the right one from the dropdowns, or click a row in the sheet. Your choices stick to that row even if rows are inserted above it. Click Save to apply.

PCR → channel mapping

Each PCR letter from the sheet records on every LSP channel whose name contains the match text as a whole word — e.g. “PCR A” matches “01 - PCR A PGM (x264)” and “02 - PCR A CLEAN (ProRes422)”, but not “PCR AB”. Channels are looked up live on every pass, so added or renamed channels are picked up automatically.
